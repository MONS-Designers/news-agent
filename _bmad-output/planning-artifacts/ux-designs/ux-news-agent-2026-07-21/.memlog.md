- (event by sally) Diagnosed the real reason v3 (single sparkle, own-center rotation, 2.4x) doesn't fully work: the sparkle glyph is 4-fold symmetric (its 4 points are identical), so a full rotation repeats its own silhouette every 90 degrees - at 14px inline size this reads as a wobble/shape-change (especially at the 45-degree 'diamond' position) rather than a clean continuous spin. This is a structural problem with rotating this specific glyph, not a tuning problem - informs why round 2 explores non-rotation directions rather than re-tuning speed/scale again.
