HybridSpinner - direction exploration, round 2 (2026-09-08)

v3 (single sparkle rotating around its own center) was the live-session baseline - user verdict "better but not perfect, need to work on this seriously." Two real problems with rotating that shape: the sparkle is 4-fold symmetric (top/right/bottom/left points are identical), so a full rotation repeats its own silhouette every 90° - at 14px inline size this reads less like a clean spin and more like a wobble, since intermediate angles (the 45° "diamond" position) look like a shape change rather than continuous rotation. It's also a solid filled glyph with no varying visual weight around the rotation axis, so it lacks the cue classic ring/arc spinners rely on to read as "turning" at all. The candidates below try genuinely different motion primitives, not just re-tuned rotation.

Scale 0.82→1.08 + opacity 0.55→1, ease-in-out, no rotation at all. Sidesteps the symmetry-wobble problem entirely since there's nothing to look "wrong" mid-turn. Sparkles conventionally twinkle rather than spin in most icon systems (Gemini's sparkle, Apple's "sparkles" SF Symbol) - this is arguably the more honest motion metaphor for this specific glyph, not just a workaround. Reads clearly as "alive/busy" even at 14px. Single moving element, matches the restrained-motion principle.

Logged as worth revisiting only if a future direction retires the sparkle entirely.

An attempt to repair v3 directly rather than replace it: a small dot marks one point of the sparkle (breaking the 4-fold symmetry so rotation has a visible "leading point" to track), rotation slowed to 1.6s, plus a slow independent breathing scale so the motion never exactly repeats within a short window. Rejected: the marker dot reads as visual noise/a stray artifact at 14px rather than a helpful cue, and two overlapping animation periods (1.6s rotation + 2.6s breathing) is more moving parts than DESIGN.md's restrained-motion principle wants for one small glyph. Fixing v3's actual problem (perceptual wobble) needs a bigger structural change than tuning knobs - this candidate confirms rotation of this specific shape is the wrong primitive, not just under-tuned.
